# Own the Machine

**An open-source draft EU Regulation: a citizens' stake in automated
productivity.** Capital for all, so the dividend follows.

For months, every entry in the book's public ledger has ended with the same
question: *who owns the machine?* This repository is the answer, drafted as
law, in the open: a Regulation establishing a citizens' capital reserve that
takes a fractional stake in hyper-automated productivity operating in the
Single Market, and pays every EU adult a direct dividend from it. Not a tax on
this year's profits: a share of the asset, claimed while the asset is forming.

Why capital and not just a cheque: a dividend without capital is a benefit that
a future majority can cancel. Poland cancelled 51.5% of every pension account's
units with one article of law. Denmark turned a frozen 1978 pay rise into an
inheritable, tax-free asset worth 27 times its original value. The difference
was never the money. It was the rule.

## Status

**Private drafting phase.** Nothing here is filed, announced or final. The text
earns its public moment by being good. See `campaign/GATES.md` for the staged
plan and its kill criteria.

## Layout

- `regulation/` — the law: recitals, articles, explanatory memorandum
  (including its own counter-arguments, written first)
- `evidence/` — the verified European evidence base the memorandum cites
- `translations/` — 24 official languages, machine-first plus adversarial gate
- `simulator/` — client-side dividend simulator, embeddable, no backend
- `site/` — campaign hub at ownthemachine.eu; links out to the Commission's
  Central Online Collection System and collects nothing itself
- `campaign/` — gates, distribution plan, naming record, organiser kit

## Declared interest

This initiative grew out of research for the book *The Horse Is Here to Stay*
(David Vanheeswijck, 2026), whose standing question gives this project its
name. Every campaign-relevant argument is free online in five languages; nobody
needs to buy anything to evaluate this proposal.
