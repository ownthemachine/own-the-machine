# Drafting brief: the September session

Written 18 August 2026 so drafting can begin cold in early September.

## What exists when you arrive

Evidence verified and imported (evidence/), gates and kill criteria set
(campaign/GATES.md), licensing decided, distribution planned, the vision and
feasibility reasoning preserved (campaign/). The name is the book's standing question answered; the tagline (capital for
all, so the dividend follows) answers the first hostile question, UBI-with-
extra-steps, before it is asked.

## The drafting order

1. **memorandum/counter-arguments.md FIRST.** The strongest case against, in
   its best form: the European wage share has not collapsed (AMECO, verified);
   Ireland's NPRF was liquidated into a bank rescue; Estonia's exit wave;
   Humlum-Vestergaard's precise nulls; "this is a tax with extra steps";
   Art. 345 TFEU and Charter Art. 17 property objections. If the articles
   cannot survive this file, stop improving the prose and fix the instrument.
2. **The four design articles**, one per test from the book, drafted against
   the actual failure statutes in evidence/europe-remedies:
   - Assets not flows (the warrant/reserve mechanism, drafted to survive
     characterisation as a fiscal measure: warrant, not levy)
   - Universal (every EU adult, no means test, no committee)
   - Raid-proof (drafted clause-by-clause against Poland Art. 23(1) and
     Spain's retrofitted RDL 2/2023; Denmark's LD lock-to-60 and Ireland's
     forfeit-on-opt-out as the commitment devices; Greece's HCAP as the
     overcorrection to avoid)
   - In time (application to undertakings above the micro-giant thresholds,
     defined by revenue-per-employee, before listing events)
3. **Recitals** from evidence/EUROPE.md's ranked numbers, each recital
   carrying its source.
4. **Severability layering** for partial registration: the ask that survives
   trimming is "assess and propose instruments for citizen participation in
   automated productivity gains"; the full Regulation sits above it.
5. **The Gate 1 letter** to the ECI Forum: warrant versus levy, Art. 114
   versus 115, partial-registration fallback.
6. **simulator/SPEC.md**: client-side only; inputs (Single Market automated
   revenue, reserve rate, eligible adults); outputs (per-citizen EUR/yr, fund
   pool); every parameter with an honest uncertainty note; output card
   designed shareable from day one (locale-aware, campaign livery).
7. **GOVERNANCE.md**: the four tests as merge criteria; a PR that makes the
   instrument less universal or less raid-proof fails review by definition.
   This file is the genuinely novel object; give it real thought.

## Register and style

British English, no em-dashes, no serial comma. The memorandum keeps the
Stable's discipline: state the strongest objection fairly before answering it,
and say plainly what the instrument cannot do (positional goods; identity).
EU legal drafting register for the articles; the FAQ layer comes later and
reuses the Questions-essay genre.

## Explicitly out of scope for the September session

Announcements, the domain going live, committee recruitment, filing anything,
paid anything. The text earns its public moment by being good.
