# Editor disposition (21 August 2026)

Two gaps were found by asking what research would improve the instrument
rather than what was left on the list. Both were real, and one of them was
the argument a well-informed opponent makes first.

## What was added

**Objection 19, the Swedish wage-earner funds.** Nothing in either
repository mentioned Rudolf Meidner or the Löntagarfonderna. That is the
closest thing anyone has attempted to the mechanism in Article 5: the 1976
Meidner plan required profitable undertakings to issue new shares worth
20 % of annual profits to collectively held funds, each year, until those
funds held a majority. The compulsory issuance was abandoned before the
1983 legislation, which levied a tax on profits and the wage sum instead,
capped in aggregate and limited to 8 % of any undertaking. Swedish business
mobilised on 4 October 1983 at a scale without modern parallel, and a
change of government abolished the funds in 1991.

It is stated at full strength because it is genuinely damaging: in the most
union-friendly economy in the world, at the height of labour's power, this
mechanism proved unlegislatable and converted into the fiscal measure that
objection 2 exists to deny. The answer rests on three differences that map
onto the reasons it failed, and refuses to claim they make the politics
safe. The 1991 abolition is also added to evidence section 4 beside Poland,
Spain and Ireland, because it is a raid on exactly this kind of fund.

**Article 16 authorities in objection 1.** The file conceded that the BRRD
line assumes failing banks and cannot carry a permanent regime for healthy
going concerns, then left the concession unanswered. Sky Österreich
(C-283/11) fills part of it.

## What the gate changed

**The first draft over-stretched Sky Österreich, and deserved what it got.**
It was written as though the case answered the Article 17 problem. Hostile
counsel called that "intellectual fraud that any EU court will dismantle in
minutes", which is right: Sky Österreich was decided under Article 16, the
freedom to conduct a business, on a narrow access obligation where the
rights-holder kept title. The passage now says so in terms, states that the
case is not authority for a taking of equity, and confines it to the
Article 16 limb, which this instrument genuinely engages because compelling
an undertaking to issue shares interferes with the conduct of its business.
The Article 17 limb is left where it was, on James and Hauer, with the
honest sentence that no decided case puts a permanent, non-crisis,
uncompensated dilution of a healthy undertaking on the right side of it.

Booker Aquaculture was dropped from reliance in the same pass. It concerned
destruction of diseased stock under an animal-health regime, which is a
public-emergency justification this instrument cannot claim, and citing it
would have invited the same charge.

**DC-41 claimed passivity the articles do not carry.** Twice. The first
draft said the Reserve is "non-voting and passive"; the second said it
"never acquires a say in how the undertaking is run". Counsel answered that
Article 5(4)(b) fixes rank and Article 5(10) makes subordinating
arrangements ineffective, so the Reserve asserts claims and is no bystander.
DC-41 now says what survives: no votes, no board seats, no say in
management.

## Refused, with reasons

At round two the gate repeated the Sky Österreich attack against text that
already disclaims the use it objects to, quoting the sentence confining the
case to the Article 16 limb and answering as though it had been offered for
Article 17. That is the gate arguing with a position the file does not
hold. Not applied.

## Residue, recorded rather than fixed

Counsel's sharpest new point survives and belongs on the open list. Article
5(10) protects the Reserve's rank against subordination. New money in a
distressed recapitalisation normally demands seniority, so a rank that
cannot be subordinated constrains how a covered undertaking raises rescue or
preference capital, even though it confers no governance right and leaves
the arrangement effective between the parties to it. Counsel calls that a
veto over capital structure in substance. The memorandum now states the
tension instead of concealing it behind the word passive, and the question
of whether Article 5(10) needs a carve-out for genuine rescue financing
joins the sub-threshold transferee under Article 5(11) and the five acquis
interface points on the list for the next drafting pass.

## One thing worth keeping

The ridicule the gate generated is the best argument for having written
objection 19 at all: "The EU's grand innovation for the AI era is a
50-year-old rejected Scandinavian antique." That line will be written by
someone eventually. It is better for it to appear first in this file, with
the answer beneath it.
