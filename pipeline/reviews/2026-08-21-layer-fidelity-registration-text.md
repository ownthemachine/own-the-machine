# Editor disposition (21 August 2026)

The registration text is new. It exists because the Article 6(3)(c) test
is applied to the words entered in the Commission's form, not to the
draft Regulation, and until now the project had the draft but not the
words. Decomposition rule 5 of the severability memorandum already fixed
the strategy: Layer 0 as the primary ask, the Regulation in the
accompanying-draft slot, so that a partial registration under Article
6(4) trims the annex and not the objectives.

Three rounds. The gate earned its place, because the first draft did
precisely what the rule forbids.

## What the gate caught

**Round 1, three findings, all accepted.** The objectives had imported
Layer 3's individual entitlement ("equal, personal and non-transferable")
and Layer 2's company-law mechanics ("settled in shares rather than cash
and entering no public budget") into the primary ask. Article 6(4)
permits partial registration only where part of the initiative
"including its main objectives" survives, so anything in field 2 is
something a registration officer can refuse rather than trim. Both were
moved to the annex, which already carried them. The gate also caught the
ask being framed as an enactment mandate ("to establish harmonised
criteria") while the annex reassured the Commission that the ask was
only an assessment and a proposal: a contradiction visible to anyone
reading both fields, now resolved in favour of Layer 0's own wording.

**Round 2, three findings, all accepted.** Objective 2 still asked for a
share of capital value to be "held on behalf of citizens of the Union",
which is the Reserve architecture under another name and the part
standing on Article 352; it now tracks Layer 0's "become broadly owned
by, or shared with". The title and chapeau had drifted from
"productivity gains" to "capital value", narrowing the ask to equity
redistribution at exactly the moment the internal-market framing is
doing the work. Third, and the one worth keeping in view for every
public-facing text: the annex described citizens' "entitlements to its
distributions" without saying that nothing is redeemable on demand and
that distributions arrive only when a liquidity event or an extraction
pattern realises value. A citizen reading the register could have taken
that for an income stream. It now says what it is.

**Round 3: PUBLISH**, no findings.

## Character limits

Verified against Annex II to Regulation (EU) 2019/788, which counts the
objectives and the annex *without spaces*: title 79 of 100, objectives
944 of 1 100, annex 3 048 of 5 000. The margin is deliberate, since the
figures are re-verified before filing and the text is translated into
every language it is registered in.

## Standing

DRAFT, not filed. Filing is a Gate 2 action and the kill criteria in
campaign/GATES.md stand unchanged. The immediate purpose is Gate 1: every
sounding will ask what the ask says, and this is the answer.
