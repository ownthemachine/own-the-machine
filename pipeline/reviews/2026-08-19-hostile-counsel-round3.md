SUBJECT: Lethal Deficiencies in Draft "European Citizens' Capital Reserve" Regulation

As instructed, I have reviewed the draft Regulation. The text is a legally suicidal manifesto masquerading as an internal market instrument. It attempts to enact a pan-European wealth tax and universal basic income via Article 114 TFEU by simply declaring that it is doing neither. 

Here are the vectors to kill it, in order of lethality.

### 1. REGISTRATION KILLS (For the Legal Service)

**ATTACK 1.1: The Article 114(2) TFEU "Fiscal Provision" Trap**
*   **Verbatim from text:** Recital 4: *"The appropriate response is not a tax... The response chosen by this Regulation operates on assets instead..."* / Article 5(2): *"subscribe at nominal value for newly issued shares representing 3 % of the fully diluted capital..."*
*   **The Motion/Argument:** Internal Note to the College of Commissioners recommending refusal of ECI registration / Action for Annulment (Art 263 TFEU). The drafter’s desperate insistence that this is "not a tax" is legally irrelevant. A mandatory, uncompensated transfer of 3% of corporate equity to a state-created entity for public distribution is a levy on capital. Article 114(2) TFEU expressly excludes "fiscal provisions" from the ordinary legislative procedure. This requires unanimity under Article 115 TFEU. The draft is ultra vires and constitutes a Treaty change in disguise.
*   **Confidence of Success:** 99% (Legal Service will instantly block this; no Member State will accept QMV on a wealth tax).

**ATTACK 1.2: Unlawful Delegation of Treaty Powers (The "Off-Budget" Violation)**
*   **Verbatim from text:** Article 8(2): *"Its assets and income shall not constitute revenue of the Union... and shall not be entered in the general budget of the Union..."* / Article 10(1): *"Every citizen of the Union... shall hold an entitlement..."*
*   **The Motion/Argument:** Refusal of registration. The EU possesses no conferred power to create a shadow sovereign wealth fund outside the EU budget (violating Article 310 TFEU's principle of universality) to pay a universal dividend directly to citizens. This bypasses the structural funds, the social policy competence limits (Article 153 TFEU), and the Own Resources Decision. 
*   **Confidence of Success:** 95%.

### 2. LITIGATION KILLS (For the Corporate Coalition)

**ATTACK 2.1: Article 17 Charter Expropriation (The "Bank Resolution" Fallacy)**
*   **Verbatim from text:** Recital 13: *"This Regulation interferes with the right to property... less intrusive than measures already upheld in the field of bank resolution."* / Article 5(2): *"subscribe at nominal value..."*
*   **The Motion/Argument:** Application for Annulment / Preliminary Reference on Validity (Art 267 TFEU). Forced dilution of 3% of equity at nominal value is an uncompensated expropriation of private property. The drafter’s reliance on bank resolution (BRRD) is a fatal false equivalence. Bank resolution bails-in shareholders of *failing* institutions to prevent systemic financial collapse. This Regulation expropriates property from *healthy* companies solely to redistribute wealth. The CJEU has never upheld uncompensated expropriation of healthy private enterprises for social engineering.
*   **Confidence of Success:** 90%.

**ATTACK 2.2: Article 345 TFEU (System of Property Ownership)**
*   **Verbatim from text:** Recital 8: *"This Regulation therefore leaves untouched the rules in Member States governing the system of property ownership..."* / Article 5(6): *"Articles 49, 68(1), (2) and (3) and 72 of Directive (EU) 2017/1132... and any corresponding provisions of the law of a Member State conferring pre-emption rights... shall not apply..."*
*   **The Motion/Argument:** Action for Annulment. The text contradicts itself fatally. It claims to respect Article 345 TFEU, yet Article 5(6) explicitly disapplies core national corporate property laws (pre-emption rights, shareholder voting on issuance, minimum consideration). Forcing a company to issue equity to a public entity fundamentally alters the national system of property ownership.
*   **Confidence of Success:** 85%.
