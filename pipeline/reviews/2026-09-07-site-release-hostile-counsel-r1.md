## 1. Registration kills (Legal Service desk)

**1.1 Fiscal-by-effect reading of the ask, Art. 114(2) TFEU.** PRE-EXISTING/DISCLOSED. [ULA]
- Verbatim (registration text, objective 2): "to propose mechanisms by which the gains and value of such production become broadly owned by, or shared with, citizens of the Union"; annex: "its possible classification as a fiscal measure remains contested despite its settlement in shares".
- Attack: "shared with" is a transfer ask; "broadly owned" via a compulsory issuance to a Union body for a public purpose is a financial contribution imposed by public authority, in kind. Refuse objective 2 under Art. 6(3)(c), register 1 and 3.
- Defence: the registration test is "manifestly outside"; the Forum reply (recorded verbatim) says goals are the unit and mechanism contestability is irrelevant; the wealth-tax ECI was registered (Decision (EU) 2023/1487, cited but not supplied — [EXT]). Objective 2 is numbered and severable; partial registration is the fallback the text itself invites.

**1.2 Asks outside conferred powers / Treaty change in disguise.** PRE-EXISTING/DISCLOSED. [ULA]
- Verbatim: Art. 8(1) "The Reserve shall have legal personality"; Art. 10(1) "Every citizen of the Union ... shall hold an entitlement". Registration text: "No amendment of the Treaties is sought."
- Attack: a Union body outside the budget owing 350 million people a direct property right is a new legal form (cf. the SCE line the severability memo itself cites, C-436/03) — Art. 352 at best, and Art. 352 requires the objective to be within Treaty policies.
- Defence: Art. 352 is expressly named as residual basis; the 5 September reviewer's Treaty-change instinct is answered by the one-sentence disclaimer on the first screen. The ECI veteran's remark is external feedback, not a Commission view.

**1.3 Non-severable drafting preventing partial registration.** NOT FOUND in the registration text; the three objectives are independently numbered and the draft act sits in the Annex II draft-act slot. One drift point [VTD, law, PRE-EXISTING]: the severability memo places Article 1 in Layer 1, yet Article 1(2) governs distributions "solely by Article 8 and Annex II" (Layer 3) and Article 1(3)(b)-(e) establishes the warrant, Reserve and entitlement — so a Layer 1 standing alone contains a subject-matter article describing bodies that no longer exist, contrary to decomposition rule 2. This matters in legislative procedure, not at registration.

## 3. Structural kills

**3.1 Obligations without an addressee inside the Reserve.** PRE-EXISTING/DISCLOSED (REVIEW-STATUS concern 2). [VTD, law]
- Verbatim: "The Reserve shall declare a distribution" (Art. 10(6)); "The Reserve shall publish annually audited accounts" (Art. 8(6)). No organ, appointment, liability or financing article exists; Art. 9(1)(e) permits subscription borrowing from nobody in particular.
- Defence: Article 9 was deliberately conduct-only; the governance candidate is on the record as not adopted. Honest, but the site's memorandum 5.3 says so, which is the right disclosure.

**3.2 Restructuring playbook (how I would advise a client).** Mostly NEW as a consolidated route map; individual limbs appear in the Fable/3.5 disposition.
1. **Starve the presumption of a fair market value.** Annex I point 4 needs an independent ≥2% transaction, an independent valuation within 12 months, or a listing. Stay unlisted; raise through convertible instruments and sub-2% tranches; take no third-party valuation. Art. 3(8) disregards what "artificially reduces the fair market value"; it says nothing about the absence of a measurement, and Art. 3(3) obliges notification "within two months after those thresholds are met" — thresholds the client cannot compute. Result: no presumption; the Commission must run a 12-month Art. 4 investigation. Delay, not immunity. [VTD as to the gap; ULA as to outcome]
2. **Buy the ratio down with insider stock compensation.** Annex I point 3 counts "share-based compensation measured at grant value"; Art. 3(8) protects "an increase in remuneration for genuine work, including an increase without additional hours or employees ... solely because it changes the ratio". Granting founders/staff ~1.25% of value per year in equity, averaged over two years, holds the ratio below 80 at a dilution cost lower than the 3% warrant, with the value staying inside the cap table. [VTD: protected on the face of 3(8); ULA whether "genuine work" survives scrutiny]
3. **Build the preference stack in the 18-month window.** Art. 18(2): "A designation decision adopted during that period shall take effect on the date of general application"; Art. 5(4)(b) ranks the Reserve only with classes "created after the designation". Every class created before general application outranks the Reserve, by design (DC-36). [VTD] Defence: DC-36 is a deliberate policy choice; closing it needs a constitutional decision, as the record says.
4. **Widen the extraction denominator.** Annex I point 2 lets the undertaking attribute bundled consideration "on a consistent and documented basis reflecting their relative stand-alone value" — the same undertaking that must stay under 25% of that figure (Art. 5(3)(a)). [VTD]
5. **Exploit the group-perimeter contradiction.** Art. 2(13) counts transfers "to undertakings within the same group" as extraction, but Art. 2(1) defines 'undertaking' to include all linked enterprises and Annex I point 1 excludes intra-group transactions — so a transfer to a parent is arguably inside the undertaking, not out of it. [VTD, ambiguity] Defence: 2(13)'s "persons controlling it" limb still catches upstream payments.

**3.3 Enforcement without an enforcer.** PRE-EXISTING/DISCLOSED. Art. 13(1)(d) fines "incorrect, incomplete or misleading information", but no article confers a request-for-information or inspection power; the Commission acts "on the basis of the facts available" (Art. 3(3)). The DMA analogue has Arts. 21-23; this draft does not. [VTD]

**3.4 Powers architecture (Arts. 290/291).** Broadly sound: CSDR-pattern Art. 15, comitology Art. 16, list-by-implementing-act and appointment-by-decision under Art. 6(1). Residual [ULA]: Art. 3(9) may amend Annex I "methodology" but not "the thresholds"; since the operative ratio is defined by Annex I points 3-4, a methodology change is a threshold change in effect — a C-355/10 essential-elements argument.
