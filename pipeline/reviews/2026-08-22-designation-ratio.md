# Editor disposition (22 August 2026)

The designation-ratio package: the repair for the findings of the same
morning, that Article 3's test designated two undertakings, missed every
integrated incumbent by factors of two to fourteen, carried an inverted
anti-avoidance rule, turned on an unpublished self-computed number, and
left Article 1 with nothing the necessity limb of Article 52(1) could be
tested against.

## What now stands

Article 3(2)(b): an undertaking is presumed decoupled where its fair
market value equals or exceeds eighty times its annual worldwide
compensation of labour, both from audited accounts. Article 1(2): the
objective, stated as ownership, a stake per citizen of the order of six
months of median equivalised disposable income within a generation, with
the derivation that three per cent is the smallest integer percentage
capable of approaching it, and an assessment clause that points down as
readily as up. Article 14(1)(e): the labour share monitored in both
directions, moving no obligation. Article 5 untouched: flat 3 %,
non-voting, whole undertaking.

On August 2026 figures the presumptively designated set moves from two
undertakings and EUR 1,5 trillion to about nine undertakings and EUR 20
trillion, spanning the United States, Taiwan and, near the threshold, the
Union itself. Stake per adult within a generation: about EUR 8 400 against
EUR 134 under the old test.

## Two designs died before this one, in private, which is the point

**The decoupled-base draft** put the decoupling measure into the quantum
(warrant over market value minus capitalised payroll) and floated the
percentage with a displacement statistic. Killed at 95 to 98 %: a rate
that moves with a macroeconomic variable is a fiscal stabiliser, and a
levy on firm A because the whole economy's labour share fell severs the
causal nexus Article 17 requires. Lesson kept: decoupling enters
designation only; every operative number stays fixed.

**The floating-factor draft** set the threshold at twenty times a Union
capitalisation factor published annually. Killed at 92 %: a threshold
moved by the market capitalisation of five hundred unrelated third
parties, published after the financial year closes, fails legal
certainty, and Eurostat cannot compute the factor as drafted. Lesson
kept: the number is fixed in the enacting terms, dimensionless, derived
in recital 7 from the observed break in the distribution (labour-reliant
undertakings reach the thirties; decoupled undertakings cluster at
seventy and above), reviewed under Article 14.

**Round two also narrowed the denominator** from all contracted labour to
own compensation of employees, after counsel showed an undertaking cannot
be required to know its vendors' internal labour costs. The narrowing
flipped the incentive for free: contracting labour out now raises the
ratio towards designation, so the only route down is direct employment.

## The acquisition exploit, and why the answer is structural

Counsel's best surviving attack: a designated undertaking acquires any
labour-heavy business and its consolidated ratio collapses below eighty,
with commercial logic genuine enough to defeat purpose-based abuse
doctrine (Halifax, Cadbury Schweppes). The original segment-based test
had been chosen precisely to close this route, as the old drafting note
records. The answer is now the conjunction of three provisions rather
than one: Article 3(8) is effects-based ("one of the main effects"),
which reaches what Halifax cannot; Article 4(3) repeals a designation
only for failure of the qualitative criteria of Article 3(1) over two
years, never for the presumption ratio alone, and refuses repeal where
the failure results from a 3(8) arrangement; and Article 3(6) designates
on the facts below the presumption. Whether a segment limb should return
as a second presumption if pre-designation dilution is observed in
practice is recorded in the Article 3 drafting note as an open point.

## Round three on the merged text: mechanical debris, all applied

Article 1's new paragraph was renumbered from 1a to 2 with the following
paragraphs shifted, because alphanumeric paragraphs belong to amending
acts, not basic acts (JPG 4.4); every reference in the memorandum and its
four translations moved with it. Recital 6's "must" became "should"
(JPG 10.1). Article 3(8)'s tail targeted turnover that point (b) no
longer contains; it now targets payroll inflation and valuation
suppression. Annex I point 2 no longer feeds a threshold and is
re-purposed for the Article 3(1)(a) assessment and market
investigations, avoiding a renumbering cascade. The Annex I drafting
notes described the dead headcount design and now describe this one.

## Refused or held, with reasons

Hostile counsel's standing conclusion, that the only legally sound
posture is no instrument, is noted and not actionable; it returns
kill-level confidence against every design shown to it, including the
current one it was reviewing favourably against a week ago. The Article
114(2) characterisation risk is real, unchanged by this package, is
objection 2's subject, and is what Gate 1's Forum reply speaks to. The
circularity of choosing the Article 1(2) target is stated in objection
21's rewritten answer rather than engineered around, because a
legislative objective is chosen, not derived, and pretending otherwise
is the vice the objection names.

layer-fidelity's DC-14 disclosure requirement, that the plain layer say
distributions start near zero and take a generation, is substantially
carried by the simulator ("the early years hug zero on purpose", the
generation sentence, the stake-beside-payout line). A plain-language FAQ
restating it is recorded as a site follow-up rather than blocking this
merge.
