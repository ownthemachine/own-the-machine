## Blocking (requires revision before the French version is sent)

**1. "Citizen payments would depend on available income" / "Les versements aux citoyens dépendraient des revenus disponibles" — verified text defect (ambiguity that inverts a core feature).**
The intended referent is the Reserve's realised income (Article 8(4), Annex II). Neither sentence names the Reserve. In the English, "available income" can be read as the citizen's income. In the French sending version, *revenus disponibles* is the ordinary statistical term for household disposable income (external proposition; not verifiable from the supplied files, but the file's own Article 1(2) uses "revenu disponible équivalent médian" in exactly that sense), so a Belgian MEP office will most naturally read it as a means test — the opposite of Article 10(2) and of the site's "no means test" line. This is the one sentence in the letter describing what citizens get, and it can be misread.
*Fix:* "would depend on the income the reserve actually realises" / "dépendraient des revenus effectivement perçus par la réserve" (and, optionally, "sans condition de ressources").
*Defence of existing wording:* the following clause ("ni leur montant ni une augmentation annuelle ne sont garantis") signals uncertainty about amounts, not eligibility. Insufficient: it does not remove the means-test reading.

**2. "The contribution level … still need[s] justification" / "Le niveau de contribution" — recommended fix, borderline blocking.**
The instrument is a 3 % equity subscription, not a contribution; Article 8(3) uses "contribution" only to exclude budget flows. The project's own record (EXTERNAL-REVIEWS review 5; counter-arguments status note) holds that three independent readers reached for "tax" first and that *presentation* must now answer the fiscal reading. A first-approach letter that calls the percentage a "contribution level" hands the recipient that reading unprompted. Not a contradiction with the file's facts; a contradiction with the file's adopted presentation rule.
*Fix:* "the size of the stake" / "le pourcentage de capital retenu".
*Defence:* the same sentence says "compulsory equity mechanism", so an attentive reader will connect them. Weak for a cold email.
