### Finding 1: Normative imperative ("shall") in technical Annex

- **Verbatim quote:**
  > "Compensation paid under arrangements whose main purpose or effect is to raise the undertaking's compensation of labour without a corresponding supply of labour to the undertaking shall be disregarded. Article 3(8) applies."
  *(Annex I, Point 3, second subparagraph)*

- **Rule breached:**
  Joint Practical Guide (2nd ed. 2015), Guideline 22; pipeline/DRAFTING-RULES.md ("Cross-references and annexes: Annexes technical only: no new right or obligation may live in an annex").
