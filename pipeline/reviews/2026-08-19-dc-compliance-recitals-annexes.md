### Compliance Evaluation: Constraints DC-1 to DC-28

- **DC-1 (Internal Market Legal Basis & Harmonisation):** **SATISFIED**  
  Article 1(1): *"This Regulation lays down harmonised rules ensuring the participation of citizens of the Union in the capital value created by hyper-automated undertakings operating in the internal market."* (See also Recitals (1) and (28)).

- **DC-2 (Designation Criteria & SME Protection):** **SATISFIED**  
  Article 3(2): *"An undertaking shall be presumed to satisfy: (a) point (a) of paragraph 1, where it achieves an annual Union turnover equal to or above EUR 7,5 billion, or where its fair market value amounts to at least EUR 75 billion, and it provides the goods or services referred to in that point in at least three Member States; (b) point (b) of paragraph 1, where its annual worldwide turnover from the provision of the goods and services referred to in point (a) of paragraph 1, divided by the number of full-time equivalents engaged in that provision, each calculated in accordance with Annex I, equals or exceeds EUR 5 million; (c) point (c) of paragraph 1, where the thresholds in points (a) and (b) of this paragraph were met in each of the last two financial years."* (See also Recital (5)).

- **DC-3 (Warrant Percentage & Singularity):** **SATISFIED**  
  Article 5(2): *"...subscribe at nominal value for newly issued shares representing 3 % of the fully diluted capital of the covered undertaking determined immediately before that event."* and Article 5(4): *"A covered undertaking shall not be required to hold more than one citizens' capital warrant in respect of the same designation."*

- **DC-4 (Equity-Only / Prohibition of Cash Settlement):** **SATISFIED**  
  Article 5(3)(b): *"[The citizens' capital warrant shall] be incapable of settlement in cash or in assets other than the shares referred to in paragraph 2;"*

- **DC-5 (Liquidity Event Trigger & No Pre-Event Operational Interference):** **SATISFIED**  
  Article 5(2): *"The citizens' capital warrant shall entitle the Reserve, upon the first liquidity event following issuance and only upon such an event..."* and Article 5(3)(d): *"[The citizens' capital warrant shall] impose no obligation on the covered undertaking prior to a liquidity event other than the notification obligation in paragraph 5."*

- **DC-6 (Third-Country Undertakings & Level Playing Field):** **SATISFIED**  
  Article 5(4): *"Where the law governing the covered undertaking does not give effect to the first sentence, the covered undertaking shall take all measures necessary to procure a subscription of equivalent effect no later than the completion of the liquidity event."* and Article 13(3): *"...the Commission may by decision, as a measure of last resort and for no longer than the non-compliance persists, prohibit the making available of the goods and services referred to in point (a) of Article 3(1) on the internal market by that undertaking."*

- **DC-7 (Company Law Pre-emption Derogations):** **SATISFIED**  
  Article 5(6): *"Articles 68(1), (2) and (3) and 72 of Directive (EU) 2017/1132 and any corresponding provisions of the law of a Member State conferring pre-emption rights or requiring a decision of the general meeting shall not apply to the issuance of the citizens' capital warrant or to the subscription of shares pursuant to it."*

- **DC-8 (Property Interference Safeguards & Non-Suspension of Transactions):** **SATISFIED**  
  Article 5(4): *"The dilution resulting from the subscription shall not exceed the percentage laid down in paragraph 2."*, Article 6(5): *"A challenge to the valuation shall not suspend the liquidity event or the subscription."*, and Article 7(2): *"The dilution borne by the shareholders of a covered undertaking in consequence of this Regulation shall not exceed the percentage laid down in Article 5(2)..."*

- **DC-9 (Independent Post-Event Valuation & Two-Way Correction):** **SATISFIED**  
  Article 6(1), (3), (4): *"The valuer shall deliver the valuation within 20 working days of the completion of the liquidity event."* and *"Where a court finds, pursuant to Article 7, that the valuation overstated the fully diluted capital, the Reserve shall transfer back to the covered undertaking, or cancel, the shares subscribed in excess of the percentage laid down in Article 5(2). Where a court finds that the valuation understated it, the covered undertaking shall issue the missing shares to the Reserve."*

- **DC-10 (Reserve Personality & Public Budget Separation):** **SATISFIED**  
  Article 8(2): *"Its assets and income shall not constitute revenue of the Union or of any Member State and shall not be entered in the general budget of the Union or in any public budget."* and Article 8(3): *"It shall receive no contribution from, and shall make no payment to, the general budget of the Union or the budget of any Member State."*

- **DC-11 (Anti-Circumvention & Substantive Headcount / Bundling):** **SATISFIED**  
  Article 3(8): *"An undertaking shall not segment, divide, combine or restructure its activities, or acquire or dispose of undertakings, where the main purpose or one of the main effects thereof is to avoid meeting the thresholds laid down in paragraph 2. The Commission shall disregard any such arrangement when applying this Regulation."* and Annex I, points 2 and 3.

- **DC-12 (Capital Preservation Rule & Anti-Leverage):** **SATISFIED**  
  Article 8(4): *"The Reserve shall retain from its realised income in each financial year the amount necessary to preserve the real value of its capital, calculated in accordance with the methodology laid down in Annex II..."* and Annex II, point 4: *"No amount shall be distributed which is financed by borrowing, by the disposal of holdings effected for the purpose of the distribution or by the retention shortfall of a future year."*

- **DC-13 (Absolute Ban on Voting Rights & Management Participation):** **SATISFIED**  
  Article 5(3)(a): *"[The citizens' capital warrant shall] confer no voting rights, no rights of information beyond those provided in this Regulation and no right to participate in the management of the covered undertaking, and the shares subscribed pursuant to it shall be non-voting for as long as they are held by the Reserve;"* and Article 9(1)(a)–(c).

- **DC-14 (Honest Distribution Discipline / No Windfall Promises):** **SATISFIED**  
  Recital (18): *"This Regulation makes no promise of substantial early payments and should not be presented as making one; its horizon is generational."*, Article 8(4), and Annex II, point 2: *"Where the retention under point 1 equals or exceeds realised income, the distributable amount shall be zero; the shortfall shall be carried forward and retained from the realised income of subsequent years before any distribution."*

- **DC-15 (Universal Unconditional Individual Entitlement):** **SATISFIED**  
  Article 10(1)–(2): *"Every citizen of the Union who has attained the age of 18 years shall hold an entitlement under this Regulation. The entitlement shall arise by operation of law. It shall not be subject to any condition relating to income, wealth, employment, contribution history or any other personal circumstance, nor to any application, save registration for payment purposes with a national vehicle."*

- **DC-16 (Inalienability of Entitlement):** **SATISFIED**

- **DC-17 (National Vehicles, Fee Cap & Portability):** **SATISFIED**

- **DC-18 (Raid-Proofing & Sovereign Debt Acquisition Prohibition):** **SATISFIED**

- **DC-19 (Penalty Revenue Accrual to General Budget):** **SATISFIED**  
  Article 13(6): *"Fines and periodic penalty payments imposed under this Article shall accrue to the general budget of the Union. They shall not accrue to the Reserve."*

- **DC-20 (Rebuttable Presumption & Irrelevance of Market Definition):** **SATISFIED**
