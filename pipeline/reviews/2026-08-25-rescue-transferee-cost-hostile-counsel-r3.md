* **Verbatim Text:**

* **Motion / Argument:**  
  **Plea of Incompetence & Breach of Article 114(2) TFEU.** Article 114(2) TFEU expressly bars internal market legal bases from enacting provisions relating to "rights and interests of employed persons" and "fiscal provisions." By imposing mandatory equity warrants on third-party transferee entities that do not meet the market harmonisation threshold of Article 3, Article 5(11) ceases to regulate the internal market or address market distortion. Instead, it operates as an unapportioned, in-kind capital levy (a direct wealth tax on corporate balance sheets) triggered solely by the movement of assets. Because the ECI mechanism cannot register a proposal manifestly outside the powers of the Commission (Regulation 2019/788, Art. 6(4)(c)), and this drafting is non-severable from the core of Article 5, the entire ECI must be refused registration.

### 2. LITIGATION KILLS (Charter Rights, General Principles & Substantive Legality)

#### Attack 2.1: Violation of Charter Article 17 (Right to Property) and Principle of Proportionality via the Transferee Multiplier Trap
* **Verbatim Text:**  
  > *Article 5(11):* "...the transferee shall issue to the Reserve a citizens' capital warrant in accordance with paragraph 1 as if it were a covered undertaking, and the obligations of the covered undertaking under this Article shall continue in respect of the automated assets it retains. The aggregate of the subscriptions to which the Reserve is entitled in respect of the same designation, under this paragraph and under paragraph 2, shall not exceed the percentage laid down in paragraph 2 of the combined fully diluted capital of the covered undertaking and of every transferee..."
* **Motion / Argument:**  
  **Action for Annulment (Art. 263 TFEU) — Infringement of Article 17 CFR and Article 5(4) TEU (Proportionality).** Article 5(11) conscripts an un-designated, potentially small transferee into issuing a 3% warrant on its *own* equity while keeping the original transferor liable for its retained assets. The cap of "3% of the combined fully diluted capital" is structurally unworkable and disproportionate: where an undertaking transfers an automated asset to a joint venture with a third party, the third-party investors suffer uncompensated expropriatory dilution of their distinct, unrelated assets. The Regulation lacks any compensation mechanism for third-party co-investors in the transferee entity, violating the essential content of Article 17 CFR. Less restrictive means (e.g., standard tracing of proceeds or security charges over the transferred asset itself) were available and disregarded.
* **Confidence of Success:** **78%**

#### Attack 2.2: Breach of Legal Certainty: Unworkable Rescue-Financing Subordination Formula
* **Verbatim Text:**  
  > *Article 5(10):* "...and only to the extent of the new consideration provided. To the extent that the preference, ranking or other advantage conferred by the issuance exceeds the new consideration provided, the first subparagraph applies to the excess."
* **Motion / Argument:**  
  **Plea of Illegality — Breach of Legal Certainty and Legitimate Expectations.** In corporate distress financing, senior liquidation preferences and structuring protections (e.g., standard 1.5x–2x non-participating preferred or senior debt-to-equity conversion terms) do not correlate arithmetically to a cash amount; they represent *priority ranking*. By declaring that the "preference, ranking or other advantage" is void against the Reserve to the extent it "exceeds the new consideration provided," the draft introduces a legally unintelligible concept. A ranking is binary, not divisible into "excess." This forces rescue financiers to subordinate their emergency injections to an un-invested public warrant, accelerating corporate insolvency in direct contradiction to Directive (EU) 2019/1023.
