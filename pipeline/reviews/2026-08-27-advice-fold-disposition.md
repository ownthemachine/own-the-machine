# Disposition: folding the Forum advice into the memoranda

Date: 2026-08-27. Three insertions (Decision (EU) 2023/1487 in the
internal-market argument; Council Recommendation 92/443/EEC in the Article
352 account; Article 115 recorded as the advisers' fallback), through
legal-basis, hostile-counsel and, for the French memorandum, the
translation-fidelity gate.

## Applied from the rounds

- **Hostile counsel's one fresh finding was right**: "Article 115 is
  recorded as a fallback" read as this file's own concession that the
  primary basis fails. The sentence now attributes the suggestion to the
  advice, records it, and keeps the advisers' own caveat. The same
  reframing is mirrored in French.
- **French fidelity caught two forks and two calques**: the
  machine-translated memorandum said *strates* where the whole French
  corpus says *couches* (5 against 42; unified corpus-wide in the file),
  and my insertion said *le présent mémorandum* where the document calls
  itself *exposé des motifs* twelve times. *Achète une réponse* and *lit
  cette décision* replaced with French a lawyer would write.

## Standing, not adopted

- Hostile counsel's remaining kills are the standing core (fiscal
  characterisation, Titanium Dioxide bifurcation, Article 17), argued at
  objections 1 to 3; the 92/443/EEC attack ignores the text's own
  disclaimer that a recommendation creates no power.
- The legal-basis round proposed three new recitals (4a, 12a, 21a) whose
  substance the recitals already carry: recital 1 holds the divergence
  evidence, the proportionality recitals the Article 17 balancing, and the
  Reserve recitals state in terms that assets belong to beneficiaries,
  enter no Union or national budget, and are at the disposal of no
  treasury. The bundle contained only the amended memorandum sections, so
  the round read absence where there is text. Declined as duplicates, with
  this note so the next reader need not re-check.
- The French memorandum's typography (no thin spaces before double
  punctuation, "no 3" for "n° 3") is a file-wide property of the machine
  draft, recorded for a dedicated pass rather than churned inside this one.
