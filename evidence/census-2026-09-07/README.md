# Company evidence pilot, 7 September 2026

This replaces neither the old universe nor the simulator. It starts a dated,
reproducible source inventory under the current payroll-ratio rule. Two issuers
were chosen to test readily available audited employee-cost disclosures, not
to represent or exhaust the covered population. No company is designated by
this research. No coverage-total or sufficiency estimate is established.

## Why these observations do not yet decide coverage

Annex I asks for consolidated compensation including employer contributions
and share-based compensation at grant value, averaged over two years. An
accounting expense for share awards is not automatically that grant-value
measure. Two annual threshold assessments ordinarily require three annual
compensation observations because each assessment uses a two-year average.

The listed-value route uses a six-month average market capitalisation, not a
year-end snapshot; other more recent qualifying valuation events may matter.
SAP's reported EUR 290.3bn and EUR 256.0bn year-end values are stored as
observations only. Neither is accepted as the Annex-I value. The service-country
and automation tests and any rebuttal/investigation must also be checked.
Missing information remains unknown, never zero and never a confirmed exclusion.

## Reproducible audit

Run the tools repository's reference/census.py with observations.json. It
computes reported-cost two-year averages and the corresponding 80-times
breakpoints as data-audit aids only. It identifies absent reconciliations,
value series, Union service footprint and ownership-perimeter checks. It
refuses to turn this incomplete pilot into a population total or minimum stake.

For a complete census, establish and archive the candidate-universe selection
rule, including private and non-EU groups offering relevant services in the
Union. Search beyond a largest-listed-companies list: the turnover route and
investigation route are not captured by a market-cap cutoff. Preserve explicit
unknown, below-presumption and potential-presumption statuses; none is a legal
finding. Review parent/subsidiary overlap before aggregation.

Next: source the six-month share-count/value series at each assessment date,
reconcile remuneration consistently, document group ownership and Union service
footprints, then expand the universe. Obtain missing values without fabricating
precision. Do not use a worldwide resident population as the eligible adult
EU-citizen denominator or PPS as euros. The 30-year stress model remains an
illustration until these inputs and a same-unit policy target are available.
