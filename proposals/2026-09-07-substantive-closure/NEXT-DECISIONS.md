| Warrant lapse | Decide whether repeal may extinguish an uncrystallised warrant before the seven-year backstop, or whether an accrued warrant survives. Work both timelines, including financial year end. | Earliest ordinary repeal after two qualifying-failure years plus five years generally reaches the seven-year backstop.
