## Legal-basis review citations and certainty

The reviewer cites C-201/18 as 'M-S'; the Court's record identifies
[Mydibel](https://infocuria.curia.europa.eu/tabs/redirect/juris/documents.jsf?num=C-201%2F18).
The cited [British Airways C-122/16 P](https://infocuria.curia.europa.eu/tabs/redirect/juris/liste.jsf?language=en&num=C-122%2F16)
concerns competition proceedings and the limits of adjudicating beyond the
relief sought. That record does not establish the review's proposed
Article 114 fiscal-classification proposition. Those citations are not
adopted for that purpose.

The need to justify the chosen legal basis and property interference is
real. [Article 114](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:12012E114)
contains the fiscal exclusion; [Article 352](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:12012E352)
provides a conditional route involving Council unanimity and Parliament
consent. This does not establish that every Reserve design must use one
particular basis, that a court would certainly annul this proposal, or that
a national-parliament objection threshold will be reached. No such outcome
prediction is endorsed here.

## Fable attack qualifications

- Its percentages of litigation/registration success are prompted estimates,
  not calibrated evidence. They are preserved only as verbatim output.
- A fixed equity fraction continues to participate in future gains; an early
  crystallisation at a low valuation does not by itself freeze the Reserve's
  monetary value forever (attack 3.5). Entity selection and apportionment are
  distinct unresolved questions.
- Genuine employee equity compensation is intentionally counted as labour
  remuneration. The examples do not establish exemption from qualitative
  designation under Article 3(6); the one-year compensation example also
  needs to apply Annex I's two-year average. Artificial grant valuations
  remain a legitimate verification concern.
- The payment-cost criticism equates all execution costs with the national
  vehicle's percentage cap. Annex II also mentions payment service provider
  charges and initial quoted costs; the claimed algebraic cancellation is
  not established for every case.
- Country-specific withholding rates, market yields, ordinary trading
  turnover and numerical success claims were not independently verified for
  this package. Do not insert them as established facts.
- A surviving drafting concern does not become an institutional decision.
  All model role headings are simulated. Fable's self-written date and round
  number are not provenance; the router timestamp and actual filename are.

The pipeline prompts now request explicit source qualifications and prohibit
invented numerical court probabilities or institutional attribution in
future runs. Existing outputs and their original prompt versions remain
preserved; changing a prompt does not retrospectively change its review.
