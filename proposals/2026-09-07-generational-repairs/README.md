# Generational ownership: review package

Status: working branch, not adopted or published. Initiator authorised this
package on 7 September 2026 after confirming the published book's thesis:
ownership established early, capital accumulated over a generation, equal
participation in available distributions, no guaranteed yearly increase.
Law baseline: 862704e. Tools baseline: 0dad61b. Site baseline: 35e057c.

## Issue and change class

This local issue record precedes the package review. No public issue or
message to others has been sent. GOVERNANCE.md normally starts drafting in a
public issue; the initiator's instruction authorises preparation now. Public
submission and final editorial acceptance remain separate. This records the
process deviation rather than pretending an issue was opened.

Substantive: lint and all six gates. Touches DC-1 to DC-3, DC-8, DC-11 to
DC-14, DC-19 to DC-24, DC-28 to DC-30, DC-33 to DC-40 and DC-45/46. No
threshold, percentage, Article 1 objective or DC table is changed. The
constitutional ambiguity in DC-1's older 'future value' description is
recorded for the editor: the subscription uses the whole capital at the
statutory event, not an incremental-gains base. Changing that DC's wording
would be a constitutional amendment, not a silent interpretation here.

## Candidate repairs in regulation/

- Article 3(8) protects genuine pay rises for existing work and genuine
  valuation reductions. Acquisitions remain under the existing effects
  test; changing that rule requires a separate policy decision.
- Article 5 makes the warrant arise at designation, with documentation
  following. Crystallisation still waits for a statutory event. Missing
  documentation and a transfer do not restart the clock. Equivalent foreign
  subscription execution follows valuation; arrangements exist by the event.
- Article 5 distinguishes dormant subscription from reporting and other
  statutory duties. Extraction and time triggers receive reporting duties;
  provisional records allow valuation to proceed before annual audit.
- Article 13 expressly covers execution, reporting and bound transferees.
  Existing maxima remain; fault, proportionality, hearings and review remain.
- Article 18 starts preparatory powers early, with designation effective
  only at general application and no retrospective capture of earlier events.
- EN/FR memoranda and relevant recitals explain the actual mechanism.

## Concrete choices for the editor

The following complete drafting proposals are separate from regulation/ so
that a review does not silently adopt a new institutional or accounting
policy. Reviewers receive them with the full operative candidate.

1. [Reserve administration](governance-candidate.md): five independent
   trustees, a board-appointed executive, accountable automation, no new
   investee-company control rights. Alternative: an existing institution
   under a verified mandate. Appointment route, seat and start-up finance
   remain explicit approval points.
2. [Issuer and payment rules](delivery-candidate.md): parent-level issuance
   with anti-double-counting safeguards; one coordinated payment registration
   and a common eligibility-period rule. Economic-unit scope is retained.
3. [Accounting specification](accounting-candidate.md): a conservative,
   cash-conserving reference model with separate appreciation, realised
   income, borrowing, costs, loss recovery and deferred allocations.

The reference model is a candidate policy implementation, not a claim that
existing Annex II already says all this. Integrating it would require a
reviewed Annex II/Articles 8–11 amendment and rerunning the Article 1 sizing
exercise under DC-45/46. The present 3% is retained; no new sufficiency claim
is made from the reference scenarios.

## Review and release discipline

Every call receives full legal context, proposals and model/specification.
Bundles are captured before submission with SHA-256 manifests. All verdicts
are preserved, including REVISE. A gate that identifies a reserved policy
choice or an unresolved legal-basis objection is recorded as open; later
gates may inspect the package with that qualification, never as a claim of
release approval. The result is a reviewable repair package, not a promise
that AI review establishes legal validity.

No proposal is merged or deployed by the coding assistant. The final editor
retains the decisions assigned by GOVERNANCE.md. See REVIEW-STATUS.md for
actual gate results and dispositions when the run completes.
