# Issuer and payment coordination: concrete choices

Draft additions for review; not yet part of regulation/. These choices
complete the operational design without replacing national payment rails.

## Proposed Article 3(10): legal issuers

10. The designation decision shall identify the legal person required to
issue the warrant and shares under Article 5. Where the undertaking is a
group, that person shall be its ultimate parent issuing equity representing
the group's consolidated economic interest. The decision shall state the
ownership perimeter and treatment of outside minority interests. No
subsidiary interest already represented in that equity shall be counted a
second time. Where no such parent equity exists, the decision shall identify
the constituent legal issuers and allocate the obligation among them by
independent valuation under Articles 6 and 7, without increasing the aggregate
interference under Article 5(2). Each issuer shall be bound by Articles 5 to
7 and 13. The decision shall be preceded by the hearing in Article 4(4).

This is a proposed default, not authority to invent shares in a legal form
that has none. A final provision needs an express equivalent-equity rule
for such a form or a justified limit; neither is silently selected here.
The group-wide designation test and Article 5(11)'s transfer rule remain.
Test minority-owned subsidiaries and common natural-person control with no
single parent; the percentage base needs value-based apportionment, not a
sum of unrelated share counts. The aggregate cap needs a worked valuation.

## Proposed Article 11 additions: payment state

6. A holder shall have one active registration for payments under this
Regulation. A national vehicle shall coordinate with other designated
vehicles to prevent duplicate payments for the same holder and period.
A transfer between vehicles shall preserve unpaid allocations, their
original period and their treatment under Article 10(4). Registration
shall not create or extinguish the entitlement.

7. A citizen residing outside the Union may register with a vehicle in a
Member State of that citizen's nationality. Where no such vehicle can
provide the service in compliance with paragraph 2, Article 11(4)'s
registration right shall apply correspondingly. Member States of nationality
shall agree responsibility for costs without delaying the holder's access.

8. National vehicles shall provide reasons for a refusal or correction,
a means to correct identity records, and an effective remedy before a court
or tribunal. A pending duplicate check shall preserve the disputed amount
pending resolution and shall not cancel it. Information exchanged shall
be limited to identifying the holder, eligibility, the relevant period and
whether payment or transfer has occurred. It shall not include income,
wealth, employment or unrelated transaction histories.

## Eligibility-period choice requiring an Article 10 amendment

Recommended candidate: allocate equal amounts per eligible day in each
calendar year, using the number of eligible holder-days as the denominator.
A holder qualifies on each day they are living, a Union citizen and at
least 18. Each day's rate is equal across the Union. A year's monetary
allocation, once determined, survives death and late payment registration
and is inheritable. Registration is a payment instruction, not the origin
of the entitlement. National vehicles retain the year/holder allocation;
the Reserve retains only the records needed for settlement and audit.

This requires explicit reconciliation with Article 10(3)'s equal amount for
the same period and Article 10(4)'s succession boundary. It is not merely a
technical implementing rule. Alternative: one common annual eligibility
date with equal annual amounts, simpler but excluding someone who dies
just before that date. The editor should choose after seeing these effects.
No means test or new residence condition is proposed.

Implementing powers, if added, should cover exchange formats, settlement
identifiers and security standards only, under a specifically conferred
Article 291 procedure. They must not choose the eligibility date, inheritance
rule or who qualifies. The current Article 16 alone does not confer them.

Acceptance scenarios: duplicate registration in two countries; a transfer
after allocation but before payment; an eighteenth birthday; death before
and after allocation; citizen living abroad; late registration; erroneous
identity collision; loss of citizenship. Payments must be idempotent and
corrections must preserve the equal entitlement for each qualifying period.
