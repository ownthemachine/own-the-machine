# Hosting an organiser introduction

Proposed operating routine, 7 September 2026. No automatic messages or
appointments are created by this document. The initiator owns the local intake
note. The hello address is forwarded by Forward Email to two project contacts'
personal Gmail inboxes; those mailbox copies are separate from the local note
and follow their own retention settings. A named person may accept local intake
responsibility separately.

## First reply and conversation

Acknowledge the specific enquiry personally. Link START-HERE and offer two
possible meeting times only after checking availability. Ask permission before
introducing the person to anyone else. Do not promise a role or signature launch.

Use a 20-minute conversation: five minutes on their questions, five on the
current draft and open issues, five to agree one small task, five to confirm
scope, time and the next contact. Reading the book is optional. Ask what support
or accessibility accommodation would help; do not require a personal explanation.

A useful contribution, reliability within the person's own agreed capacity,
respectful disagreement and willingness to disclose conflicts matter more than
audience size. Do not rank people by political beliefs, inferred vulnerabilities,
national stereotypes or likelihood of persuading their communities.

## First week

Give one task with a link, a definition of done and a named reviewer. Grant only
the access needed for that task. Do not share account passwords. Check the work
and ask whether the person wishes to continue; inactivity is not consent.
No person becomes an organiser through a status change in an internal tracker.

## Before a formal organiser decision

Separately verify eligibility under the current official rules; explain personal
responsibilities, names becoming public, representative/substitute roles,
funding, data-controller arrangements and the group's decision/exit rules.
Record explicit agreement and any needed professional advice before filing.
Do not collect passport copies until the specific need, secure handling and
retention arrangements are explained and established.

## Minimal private tracking

Store only contact name/address, enquiry date, voluntarily provided residence
if relevant to the statutory role, chosen task, agreed time, owner, next action,
sharing permission and status. Suggested statuses: enquiry, conversation,
first task, contributor, exploring organiser role, declined/closed. Formal
organiser agreement is a separate record. Do not put individual records in Git.
Review inactive local intake notes after 90 days and delete contact records no longer
needed unless the person agrees to continued contact or another retention need
has been explained. This is a proposed routine, not an already deployed system.
This review does not automatically delete copies in the project contacts' Gmail
inboxes.

## Automation boundary

Automate reminders to the intake owner, task templates and aggregate outstanding
counts. Personal replies, introductions, access grants and role commitments
require the responsible person's action. Keep a failure queue and a manual
fallback. Do not use bulk direct messages, scrape personal profiles or turn the
intake into a political persuasion score.
