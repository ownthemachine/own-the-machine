## Proposed first public post

I wrote The Horse Is Here to Stay to explore a long-term question: how can
people retain economic security as machines do more of the work?

Own the Machine is my attempt to turn that question into a concrete proposal
for lasting citizen ownership of automation-related capital. The draft and
its objections are now public.

This is a project in preparation. The legal and economic questions are still
open; it is not a registered European Citizens' Initiative, and there is
nothing to sign yet.

I would welcome unpaid volunteers who want to examine the evidence, improve the explanation
or help organise the next stage. You do not have to agree with every article.
A first conversation and one small task are enough to start.

The proposal: https://ownthemachine.eu/
How to help: https://ownthemachine.eu/join

## Reaching people without a targeting campaign

Use one open invitation available to everyone through the public project Page,
the initiator's profile and the website. Interested people can share that same
invitation voluntarily. With permission, a public meeting host can make the
same invitation available; no unsolicited private outreach is scheduled here.
Respond to people who opt in and match concrete tasks to the skills and time
they volunteer. Assess formal organiser eligibility separately from interest.
Do not infer political views from profiles or select demographic groups for
custom persuasive messages. No named prospect list is created by this pack.

## First two weeks

Publish the introduction once after editorial approval. Prepare a factual
explanation of the Reserve and a separate update on one open research question.
Offer an open question session only when a host and time are confirmed. Keep
comments and questions in a response queue; correct errors visibly. Avoid
posting frequency promises that exceed actual capacity.

Review whether enquiries receive answers, people understand the difference
between the proposal and a registered initiative, and agreed first tasks are
completed. Page views, reactions and enquiries are not signatures. Use aggregate
counts and voluntary feedback; no person-level political profiling or ads.

a full 14 days. Link it from the introduction and record the opening and closing
dates. Do not count earlier private Gate 1 soundings as a completed public window.
No such consultation is claimed to have opened by this preparation pack.

Check the Page fields, final copy, live join page and both mail routes, confirmed
