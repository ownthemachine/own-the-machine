# Funding and support

Regulation (EU) 2019/788 requires the organisers of a registered initiative
to declare their sources of support and funding, to name any source
contributing more than EUR 500 in a year, and to keep that declaration
current for as long as signatures are being collected. The obligation
starts at registration. This file starts it now, on the reasoning that a
campaign asking for a rule about who owns what should be able to say who
pays for it at any moment, and not only once a regulation compels it.

## State on 21 August 2026

There is no organisation, no bank account, no donations and no third-party
money of any kind. Nobody has been paid, and nobody has worked on this for
payment.

Saying "none" would be the easier answer and it would not be true. The site
costs something to run, and the initiator, David Vanheeswijck, pays it
personally:

- the ownthemachine.eu domain,
- object storage and edge delivery at Scaleway, in France,
- the API calls the review gates consume.

No party, foundation, trade union, company, public body or private person
other than the initiator has contributed money, services, staff time,
office space, data or software licences to this campaign.

## What changes at Gate 3

Gate 3 needs real money: an ASBL, a filing, a launch. From the moment an
account exists, this file records every source above EUR 500 with the
amount and the date, and every source below that threshold in aggregate.
Support in kind is recorded on the same terms as cash and valued honestly,
because an undeclared donation of services buys exactly what an undeclared
donation of money buys.

## Rules adopted in advance

Adopted now, while refusing costs nothing, rather than when the first
offer arrives.

- No anonymous contributions, in any amount.
- Nothing whose acceptance would require concealing the donor, the amount
  or the date.
- Nothing from an undertaking that would be a covered undertaking under
  Article 3 of the draft, from a group to which such an undertaking
  belongs, or from anyone acting on its behalf.
- Nothing from a political party, and nothing conditional on the text.
- The declaration is updated before anyone asks for it, not after.
