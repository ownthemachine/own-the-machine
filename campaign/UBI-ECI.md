# The 2026 UBI ECI: profile and comparison

Researched and verified 19 August 2026. Why this file exists: an
initiative on unconditional basic income, ECI(2026)000008, was
registered on 7 July 2026 and collects from 4 January 2027. It shares
our narrative territory (AI decoupling livelihoods from labour) and its
file contains the single most useful piece of procedural intelligence
available to us.

## The headline: refused first, registered after a one-day amendment

Commission Implementing Decision C(2026) 4721 final records that the
initiative as first submitted on 14 May 2026 "manifestly fell outside
the framework of the Commission's powers" (Article 6(3)(c) letter of
15 June 2026, C(2026) 4069 final). Under the Article 6(4) dialogue the
organisers amended the ask on 16 June, trimming it to "all necessary
and legal steps" plus a recommendation to Member States, and the
amended version was registered in full on 7 July. Refusal, one-day
amendment, registration inside three weeks.

Consequences for us:

1. **The a fortiori line for the Gate 1 letter.** The Commission
   registered a redistribution-of-automation-gains subject with no EU
   legislative competence behind it (Article 153(4) TFEU reserves
   social-security design to Member States), expressly deferring
   subsidiarity, proportionality and fundamental rights to the
   post-collection stage (recital 8). An initiative aimed at harmonised
   obligations on undertakings in the internal market, with Article 114
   TFEU available, cannot then be *manifestly* outside the framework.
2. **Pre-draft the fallback.** Their file proves the amend-and-resubmit
   dialogue works in practice. We should hold a pre-drafted amended
   formulation of our ask in reserve, so a refusal letter costs days,
   not months.
3. **Do not over-trim.** Their registered ask is now so soft that total
   success yields a recommendation. Our red line stays: the ask must
   survive as "propose a legal act"; severability and partial
   registration are the safety valve, never pre-emptive dilution.

## Profile

Registered title: "Introduction of Unconditional Basic Income (UBI)
throughout the EU". Register 2026/000008; collection 4 January 2027 to
about 4 January 2028. Organisers: the UBIE network (representative
Klaus Sambor, Austria; substitute Michele Gianella, Italy); declared
funding EUR 1 676. The annex defines UBI as universal, individual,
unconditional and "high enough" (at least 60 % of national median net
equivalent income). Financing addressed in one line: "a redistribution
of wealth from the top". The Commission registered it on its own hooks,
Articles 121(2) and 148(2) TFEU, both soft-law coordination
instruments.

Third attempt by the same network: the 2013 ECI reached about 300 000
signatures, the 2020-2022 ECI reached 296 365 (29,6 % of threshold).

## Comparison

| Dimension | UBI ECI 2026 | Own the Machine |
|---|---|---|
| Legal nature | Income floor, welfare-state expansion | Property income on a collectively owned stake |
| Funding | Taxation, unspecified ("from the top") | One-time 3 % equity warrant per designated firm |
| Who pays | Member-state budgets | Shareholders of hyper-automated firms, by dilution |
| Scope | Economy-wide | Designated firms only (Article 3 thresholds) |
| Flow | Immediate, at subsistence level | At liquidity events, into the Reserve, distributed from realised income after real capital preservation |
| Promise | At least 60 % of median income now | Small at the start, compounding, honest about it |
| Ask | Soft law (a recommendation) | Hard law (a Regulation, Article 114, Article 352 fallback) |
| Budget insulation | None | Reserve insulated from all public budgets, both directions |

## Positioning

Structurally complementary, situationally competitive. Their collection
window overlaps anything we would run; media may lump us together, and
their concrete promise makes our honesty look thin to a casual reader.
But their network proves latent demand, our mechanism can recruit
exactly the people who reject tax-funded UBI, and "they ask for a
recommendation, we bring a regulation" is a differentiator. Posture:
never attack UBI; the diagnosis overlaps and their annex is sincere.
We are the property answer to the same question, and their signers are
welcome to sign both.

## FAQ differentiation lines (feed objection 13)

1. A basic income is an income floor funded by taxation; a citizens'
   capital reserve is an ownership stake funded by equity issuance:
   you are paid because you own, not because you qualify.
2. A benefit must be re-voted in every budget and can be repealed by
   any majority; a reserve holding market assets in the citizens' name
   is insulated from every public budget and outlives the government
   that created it.
3. UBI promises subsistence from day one and must find the taxes to
   match; we promise a small dividend that compounds as automation
   compounds: less at the start, unkillable in the long run.
