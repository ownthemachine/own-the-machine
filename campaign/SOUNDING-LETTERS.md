# The Gate 1 sounding letters

Status: drafted 27 August 2026, after the Forum's answer and quoting it.
Three letters for the three channels GATES.md names. Each is complete
except its first line: OUTREACH.md requires the opening to say why this
person specifically, drafted per person once the shortlist is set, and a
templated version of that line is worse than none. Everything after the
opening is ready to send.

What each letter does, per OUTREACH.md: says a complete draft exists and
has been through adversarial review; asks for one hour on
registration-stage experience; says plainly that no money and no
endorsement are sought; points at ownthemachine.eu with the ten-minute
test. What is new since that plan was written: the Forum's independent
advice of 27 August, quoted in one sentence, conditionally as it was
given, and never oversold, because the advice is non-binding and the
recipient will know it.

One hostile round changed three things before anything ships: the
advice is quoted with its own condition rather than as "must be
registered" flat; the size of the designated set stays in the evidence
base, where it lives with its derivation, instead of in a cold letter
where it is a soundbite; and the line connecting the recipient's field
to the file was cut, because that connection belongs in the personal
opening or nowhere.

## Letter A: the researcher (ETUC/ETUI route)

> [Opening line: why this person. Their own work on worker financial
> participation, named. Drafted with the shortlist.]
>
> I am writing because a complete draft EU Regulation now exists on a
> question adjacent to that work: citizens' participation in the capital
> of undertakings whose output has decoupled from employment. It would
> require the few undertakings above high, objective thresholds to issue
> once a non-voting warrant over 3 % of their capital to a common reserve
> held for all Union citizens. The draft, its explanatory memorandum, an
> objections file that states the strongest cases against it, and the
> record of every adversarial review it has been through are public at
> ownthemachine.eu. Ten minutes there should settle whether the hour I am
> asking for is worth your time.
>
> The ECI Forum's independent legal advice service reviewed the
> registration question on 27 August and answered that, on its reading,
> registration turns on whether the goals are within Union competence,
> not on the contestability of an annexed draft act; it also noted
> Council Recommendation 92/443/EEC, the Union's earlier instrument on
> participation in enterprise results.
>
> The hour I am asking for is about the registration stage and the
> participation framing: where a proposal like this reads as serious to
> people who work on financial participation, and where it does not. I am
> not asking for money, endorsement or association of any kind, and the
> project's own published criteria stop it if it cannot assemble
> organisers and partners on the merits.
>
> David Vanheeswijck
> Belgium · ownthemachine.eu

## Letter C: the ECI veteran

> [Opening line: why this person. Their initiative, named, and one true
> sentence about what it achieved or taught. Drafted with the shortlist.]
>
> I am preparing the ground for an initiative and trying to learn from
> people who have actually carried one, before constituting an
> organisers' group rather than after. The subject is citizens'
> participation in the productivity gains of hyper-automated production;
> the instrument is a complete draft Regulation, published with its
> explanatory memorandum, twenty-one objections stated at full strength,
> and the record of every review round, at ownthemachine.eu. Ten minutes
> there should tell you whether the hour I am asking for is worth it.
>
> The registration-stage law I can read, and the ECI Forum's independent
> advice service has answered the questions I could put in writing: on
> its reading registration turns on the goals rather than on the annexed
> mechanism. What I cannot read anywhere is what you know: what the
> registration dialogue with the Commission was actually like, what
> consumed the committee's first months, what you would refuse to
> repeat, and what a single initiator most underestimates about the step
> from a text to seven organisers in seven states.
>
> That hour is the whole ask. No money, no endorsement, no use of your
> name; the project's published criteria stop it if it cannot assemble
> the people on the merits.
>
> David Vanheeswijck
> Belgium · ownthemachine.eu
