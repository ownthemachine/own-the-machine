# Gate 1 sounding: ECI veterans shortlist

Researched and verified against live pages, 19 August 2026. Purpose:
one private hour with someone who has been through ECI registration,
before constituting an organisers' group. Outreach drafts at the
bottom; personalisation line per candidate. Not yet contacted.

## Ranked shortlist

1. **Carsten Berg**, director of The ECI Campaign, ECI Help Desk
   advisor, co-host of ECI Day with the EESC. Has advised more ECIs
   through registration than anyone; campaigned for the instrument's
   insertion into the treaties. Contact: berg [at]
   citizens-initiative.eu (verified via citizens-initiative.eu/team-members).
2. **Daniela Vancic**, lead European policy, Democracy International
   (Cologne); official Commission ECI Forum Ambassador. Cross-campaign
   COCS-era vantage point. Contact: vancic@democracy-international.org
   (verified live).
3. **Tony Venables**, founder, ECIT Foundation, Brussels. Has
   registered his own ECIs in the current era (Voters Without Borders;
   All On Board) and knows the registration unit's reactions and the
   failure modes first-hand; twenty minutes from Zonhoven by train to
   Brussels. Contact: info@ecit-foundation.eu; LinkedIn
   /in/tonyvenables.
4. **Alberto Alemanno**, Jean Monnet Professor of EU Law, HEC Paris;
   founder, The Good Lobby. Strongest legal-admissibility brain on the
   list. Contact: LinkedIn /in/alemanno (active); HEC directory; The
   Good Lobby contact form.
5. **Jan Willem Goudriaan**, general secretary, EPSU; vice-president
   of the Right2Water citizens' committee, the first ECI ever to
   succeed. The redistributive-economics success story; Dutch speaker.
   Contact: EPSU site form or LinkedIn (staff page blocks bots).
6. **László Andor** (FEPS) and the Tax the Rich circle (Aurore Lalucq
   MEP). Registered July 2023, ~232 000 signatures, failed collection
   October 2024: the nearest thematic precedent, with fresh lessons on
   why an economic ECI stalls at collection. Contact: FEPS channels;
   Lalucq's MEP office.
7. **Dr Nils Kluger**, spokesperson, Stop Finning (1,1 M validated).
   Proof a volunteer-run ECI can clear a million; teaches
   collection-system mechanics in Commission ECI Forum webinars.
   Contact: ECI Forum / campaign channels.

Reserves: **Olga Kikou** (End the Cage Age; the endgame-risk
conversation; reverify current role first) and the **Stop Killing
Games** organisers (freshest full-cycle COCS success, but a very
public operation, weaker fit for a soft-confidentiality ask).

Adjacent, not advisers: a new **UBI ECI was registered 7 July 2026**
(UBIE, contact@ubie.org): thematically near the universal dividend,
which makes them near-competitors; worth tracking, not sounding.

## Personalisation lines

- Berg: "You have advised more ECIs through the Help Desk than anyone,
  and I would rather hear what the registration unit actually reacts
  to than guess it from the regulation."
- Vancic: "As an ECI Forum Ambassador who has followed the instrument
  through the 2020 reform and into the central collection era, you see
  patterns across campaigns that no single organiser does."
- Venables: "You have registered ECIs from Brussels yourself,
  including recent ones, and I am a short train ride away, so this
  could as easily be a coffee as a call."
- Alemanno: "Your citizen lobbying work sits exactly at the junction I
  have reached: a finished legal text looking for the right procedural
  entry point."
- Goudriaan (NL outreach an option): "Right2Water remains the proof
  that an economic rights ECI can reach the Commission and win
  something concrete, and as a fellow Dutch speaker I would value your
  view on what that actually took."
- Andor/FEPS: "Tax the Rich is the closest thematic precedent to my
  draft, and I would value a candid view on why collection stalled and
  what you would do differently."
- Kluger: "Stop Finning showed a volunteer-run ECI can pass a million,
  and your Forum webinars on the collection system are the closest
  thing to the briefing I am looking for."

Caveats: Kikou's current role confirmed only to about 2024; Goudriaan's
direct email not publicly verifiable; the Tax the Rich final count is
