# The name

Decided 18 August 2026, third iteration in one day, recorded because the trail
is instructive.

1. **Own the Stable** (original drafts). Rejected: requires the book's horse
   metaphor to parse; a stranger sees agriculture.
2. **Capital for All** (interim). Right idea, stated as the remedy: it answers
   "is this not just UBI?" in the name. But abstract, and faintly cold.
   Retained as the tagline, where it does that same work.
3. **Own the Machine** (final, David's call). The book's standing question,
   *who owns the machine?*, ends every ledger entry and social card already
   published; the campaign name is the answer to a question months of public
   material have been asking. A demand, which is how winning ECIs are named
   (Right2Water, End the Cage Age). Needs no context in 2026. Stays English in
   all markets, per ECI convention; the tagline localises.

Availability verified 18 August 2026: ownthemachine.eu, whoownsthemachine.eu
and ownthemachine.org all AVAILABLE at the registries; ownthestable.eu also
still available; ourshare.eu and aidividend.eu squatted. Registration of the
two .eu names authorised by David the same day, whoownsthemachine.eu as a
redirect so the question resolves to the answer.
